Frequently Asked Questions — New York — NYCIRB & WCB Rules

What is Form C-105.2 in New York?
Form C-105.2 is the official NY Workers' Compensation Board certificate proving active private-carrier coverage—required by NYC DOB before issuing building permits.
Does New York cap high construction wages on workers' comp audits?
Yes. Under NYCIRB rules, employers meeting specific construction territory/wage criteria can cap auditable weekly payroll per employee.
