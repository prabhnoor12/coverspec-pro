Actuarial Methodology

NCCI Workers’ Compensation Rating Formula

Standard Commercial Premium Equation:
Annual Premium = (Auditable Payroll ÷ 100) × NCCI Class Rate × State Factor × EMR × Schedule Credit

Lowering your Experience Modification Rate (EMR) from 1.15 to 0.85 on a $500,000 commercial construction payroll typically saves $5,000 to $22,000+ every single year while unlocking major GC bid lists that reject EMRs above 1.00.

Underwriting Q&A

Workers’ Comp & EMR Frequently Asked Questions

How is commercial workers’ compensation premium calculated?
Premium equals (Annual Payroll ÷ 100) multiplied by your NCCI class code manual rate, adjusted for state bureau factors, Experience Modification Rate (EMR), and schedule safety credits.
What is a good Experience Modification Rate (EMR) for contractors?
1.00 is industry average. Commercial subcontractors maintaining an EMR of 0.75–0.90 save 10%–25% annually and pass strict general contractor pre-qualification checks.
Does overtime pay count toward workers’ comp payroll audits?
In most NCCI jurisdictions, only the straight-time base rate of overtime hours is auditable—the extra half-time portion is excluded when clearly itemized in payroll records.
Are commercial businesses in Texas required to carry workers’ comp?
Texas allows private employers to be non-subscribers, but commercial GCs, landlords, and public contracts almost always mandate a standard Workers’ Comp Certificate of Insurance (COI).
