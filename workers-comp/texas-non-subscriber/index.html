Frequently Asked Questions — Texas — TDI & Non-Subscriber Rules

Is workers' comp mandatory in Texas for commercial contractors?
Texas state law does not mandate it for private employers, except on public/government contracts—however virtually all commercial general contractors require a statutory Texas Workers' Comp COI.
What is Texas Waiver of Subrogation endorsement WC 42 03 04 B?
It prevents your workers' comp carrier from suing the general contractor or property owner after paying an injured worker's claim; standard requirement on TX commercial subcontracts.
