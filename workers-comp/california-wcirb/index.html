Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — California — WCIRB & CSLB Mandate

What is California's WCIRB dual-wage workers' comp rule?
California splits construction trades into low-wage and high-wage class codes. Employers paying above the WCIRB hourly threshold receive significantly lower manual rates.
Does California require workers' comp for sole-proprietor contractors?
Yes for high-risk licenses (C-39 Roofing, C-20 HVAC, C-8 Concrete, D-49 Tree) and expanding statewide under SB 216.
