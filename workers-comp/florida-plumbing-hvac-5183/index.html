Frequently Asked Questions — Florida Plumbing & HVAC (5183)

What is the workers' comp rate per $100 payroll for plumbing & hvac in Florida?
Under Florida NCCI Class 5183, commercial plumbing & hvac firms typically benchmark around $3.83 per $100 of auditable payroll before EMR credits or schedule discounts.
How can a Florida plumbing & hvac company lower its workers' comp audit bill?
Separate straight-time from overtime half-time pay, verify office staff under clerical Class 8810, collect COIs from every subcontractor, and enroll in Pay-As-You-Go payroll sync.
