Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — B2B SaaS & Tech — SOC 2 & Tech E&O

Why do enterprise buyers require $2M to $5M cyber insurance from SaaS vendors?
If a SaaS vendor's cloud environment is breached, enterprise clients face downstream data exposure and regulatory fines—they transfer that vendor risk via MSA insurance minimums.
Should a SaaS company buy standalone Cyber or combined Tech E&O + Cyber?
Combined Tech E&O + Cyber on a single carrier form is best practice so two separate insurers cannot point fingers during a cloud outage or breach.
