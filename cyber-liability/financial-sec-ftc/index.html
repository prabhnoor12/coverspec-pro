Frequently Asked Questions — CPA, Finance & Legal — FTC / SEC

Does Professional Liability (E&O) cover client wire transfer fraud?
Usually no, or only with tiny $25k sublimits. Standalone cyber policies with explicit Social Engineering / Funds Transfer Fraud endorsements are required.
Does the FTC Safeguards Rule affect cyber insurance underwriting?
Yes. Carriers now ask directly on cyber applications whether your firm has a WISP, enforced MFA, and EDR—passing unlocks 15%–25% better rates.
