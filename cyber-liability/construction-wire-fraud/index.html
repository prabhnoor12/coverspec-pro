Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Construction GCs — ACH & Wire Fraud

Why do commercial general contractors need cyber insurance?
GCs wire large progress payments to dozens of subcontractors every month, making them prime targets for invoice-spoofing wire fraud and accounting-server ransomware.
Does my Commercial Crime policy cover social engineering wire fraud?
Standard Crime policies often exclude 'voluntarily parted' funds tricked via spoofed emails unless you add an explicit Social Engineering / Funds Transfer Fraud endorsement.
