Underwriting Q&A

Commercial Cyber Liability FAQs

How much does $1M to $2M of commercial cyber liability insurance cost?
Most SMBs ($1M–$10M revenue) with MFA and EDR pay $1,800 to $5,500+/year for $1M–$2M standalone cyber coverage, scaling higher for HIPAA healthcare, financial advisory, and high-record SaaS platforms.
What security controls do cyber insurance underwriters require?
Carriers require enforced Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on email and remote access, EDR software on endpoints, and immutable/air-gapped backups to bind preferred $1M–$5M limits.
Does cyber liability cover wire transfer fraud and social engineering?
Yes, via a Funds Transfer Fraud / Social Engineering endorsement (typically $100k–$250k+ sublimit), essential for construction GCs, real estate/title firms, and CFOs.
Why isn’t cyber liability covered by General Liability (GL)?
Standard ISO CGL policies exclude electronic data loss, ransomware extortion, breach notification legal costs, and regulatory fines—requiring a standalone commercial cyber policy.
