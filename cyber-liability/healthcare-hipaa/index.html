Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Healthcare & Clinics — HIPAA Cyber

Does medical malpractice insurance cover a HIPAA ransomware attack?
No. Medical malpractice covers clinical errors; standalone commercial cyber liability is required for ransomware, forensic IT, patient notification, and HHS OCR regulatory defense.
How much cyber insurance should a 30-person medical clinic carry?
Most multi-provider clinics and dental practices carry $2,000,000 to $3,000,000 aggregate limits to cover forensic legal costs, OCR defense, and 2–3 weeks of EHR downtime.
