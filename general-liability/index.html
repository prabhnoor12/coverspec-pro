ISO CGL Underwriting

Commercial GL Revenue & Subcontractor Audit Formula

ISO Commercial General Liability Equation:
Annual GL = [(Self-Performed Rev ÷ 1,000 × Trade Rate) + (Insured Sub Spend ÷ 1,000 × Sub Rate)] × State × Limit Factor

Commercial GL policies are auditable at year-end. Collecting valid Certificates of Insurance (with Additional Insured CG 20 10 / CG 20 37) from every subcontractor keeps sub spend rated at ~$1.10–$1.65 per $1k instead of full self-performed rates.

Underwriting Q&A

General Liability & Surety Bond FAQs

How is Commercial General Liability (GL) priced for contractors?
GL is rated per $1,000 of annual gross revenue (self-performed work) plus a lower pass-through rate on insured subcontractor costs, multiplied by state litigation factors and policy limits.
What is the difference between $1M/$2M GL and adding a Commercial Umbrella?
$1M/$2M is the standard commercial baseline. Stacking a $2M Commercial Umbrella over $1M/$2M GL satisfies $3M contract minimums at a lower cost than raising primary limits.
How much does a $25,000 California CSLB contractor license bond cost?
Contractors with 680+ credit typically pay 0.75% to 1.5% annually ($190–$375/year) for the mandatory $25,000 CSLB license bond.
Why is Commercial GL so expensive in New York?
NY Labor Law §§240–241 imposes strict absolute liability for elevation-related construction injuries, making NY commercial GL 50%–100%+ higher than most other states.
