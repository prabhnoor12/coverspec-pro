Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Texas — Commercial GL & Sub Audits

Does Texas require a state general contractor license bond?
Texas does not have a statewide GC license, but cities like Houston, Austin, and San Antonio require trade permits/bonds and commercial landlords mandate $1M/$2M GL.
What happens if my Texas subcontractors don't give me a COI?
At your annual GL audit, the carrier will reclassify uninsured subcontractor payments as full self-performed revenue, potentially adding thousands in surprise audit bills.
