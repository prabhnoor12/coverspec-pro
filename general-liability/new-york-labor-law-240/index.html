Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — New York — Labor Law 240/241 GL

Why is New York contractor GL insurance so much more expensive?
NY Labor Law 240 ('The Scaffold Law') holds contractors and property owners strictly liable for gravity-related jobsite injuries regardless of worker fault.
What is an Action-Over exclusion in NY commercial GL?
It strips away coverage when an injured employee sues the property owner/GC and the owner tenders the claim back to the subcontractor under contract indemnity.
