Frequently Asked Questions — California Electrical Contractor GL

How much does General Liability insurance cost for a electrical contractor in California?
Commercial electrical contractor firms in California typically pay around $5.12 per $1,000 of annual revenue for $1M/$2M GL, depending on claims history and subcontractor ratio.
What COI endorsements do California commercial owners require?
Most commercial contracts require Additional Insured (CG 20 10 ongoing & CG 20 37 completed operations), Primary & Non-Contributory wording, and Waiver of Subrogation.
