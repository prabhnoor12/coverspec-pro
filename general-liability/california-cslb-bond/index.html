Frequently Asked Questions — California — CSLB $25k Bond & GL

How much does a California $25,000 CSLB contractor bond cost?
With 700+ credit, contractors typically pay $190 to $375 per year (0.75%–1.5% of the $25,000 bond amount).
Why do California LLC contractors pay more for bonds?
California requires LLC contractors to carry an additional $100,000 LLC Employee/Worker Bond plus at least $1,000,000 in General Liability insurance.
